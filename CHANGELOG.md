# Changelog

## 1.6.0

- Added Pyro, Verdant, Void and Abyssal Spellweave weapons for T.O Magic 'n Extras

## 1.5.0

- Arcane, Mithril and Pyrium weapons come from Iron's Spellbooks, and Shimmersteel weapons come from Auroral.
- Pyrium weapons are fireproof and set targets on fire. Mithril weapons pierce armor, and Shimmersteel weapons repair themselves.
- Grips and bindings on these weapons take their colours from each mod's own weapons.

## 1.3.0

- Ignitium and Cursium weapons are smithed with Cataclysm's own upgrade templates, as in Spartan Cataclysm.
- Ignitium, Cursium and Witherite weapons are Epic and survive explosions. Witherite, Ancient Metal and Black Steel weapons smelt back into ingots or nuggets.
- Cataclysm traits play Cataclysm's own sounds and particles. The trait key cataclysm_sounds_enabled turns the sounds off.
- Mecha Smite can heal below a fixed health value instead of a fraction, and a zero wither or fire length switches that part off.
- Each material's rarity is set in materials.json.
- Trait effects have descriptions and the original Spartan Cataclysm icons.

## 1.2.0

- Trait numbers (chances, cooldowns, damage, effect levels) can now be changed by datapacks through files in data/<namespace>/spartan_compat/traits/. docs/DATA.md lists every key with its default and range.

## 1.1.0

- 23 new materials come from seven more mods, Botania, Ender IO, Psi, Eternal Starlight, Nature's Aura, Eidolon Repraised and L2 Complements.
- Each one does what its mod's own sword does. Botania weapons repair from mana, Eternal Starlight ones freeze, poison or burn, and Psi weapons take bullets and cast spells.
- Dark steel weapons behead and hold their own energy. L2 Complements tooltips use L2's wording.

## 1.0.0

First public release. Everything below is in it.

## 0.2.0

- Neptune's Might works on melee and thrown weapons and requires the target's eyes to be underwater.
- Necromium inflicts universal Slowness II for 120 ticks and upgrades diamond weapons with a netherite template.
- Ironwood and Steeleaf receive their original supported enchantments. Knightmetal checks natural armor and worn armor coverage. Fiery weapons gain blaze-rod/pole crafting, vial conversions, uncommon rarity and fire-immune particles.
- Cataclysm traits use the original chances, stacking, healing, effect lengths and pulse cooldown. Witherite returns to ordinary crafting. Balance values live in traits.json.
- Create (Brass, Zinc), Ars Nouveau (Source Gem), Occultism (Iesnium) and AE2 (Certus Quartz, Fluix) add six material sets, each with its own recoloured Spartan sprites.
- No club or cestus is added.
